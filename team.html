Learn more about the professionals behind Strata Partners

Strata Partners is a full-service commercial law firm providing strategic legal advisory, transactional support, and dispute resolution services to businesses and private clients across Nigeria and beyond. The firm is led by Managing Partner Ope Olatinwo and a team of experienced partners and associates committed to practical, results-driven legal solutions.

We combine deep legal expertise with commercial insight. Our team approaches every matter not just as lawyers, but as dealmakers and strategists who understand business realities, regulatory environments, and long-term client goals.

We advise entrepreneurs, SMEs, corporate organisations, investors, developers, agricultural businesses, and high-net-worth individuals across sectors such as real estate, infrastructure, finance, agriculture, manufacturing, and technology.

Our primary operations are in South West Nigeria, with a strategic presence along the Lagos–Ibadan corridor, allowing us to efficiently serve clients across Lagos, Ogun, and other major commercial hubs.

Ope Olatinwo

Managing Partner of Strata Partners

Ope Olatinwo is the Managing Partner of Strata Partners. He is a commercially astute legal practitioner, dealmaker, and strategist with a strong blend of advisory, transactional, and dispute-resolution experience. He is particularly known for structuring and negotiating complex transactions and for delivering pragmatic legal solutions that align with clients' commercial objectives.

He obtained his Bachelor's degree from the University of Ilorin and proceeded to the Nigerian Law School, where he was duly called to the Nigerian Bar. Upon his call, Ope commenced his professional career at Paul Usoro & Co., where he gained exposure to top-tier legal practice, including high-value corporate advisory, regulatory compliance, and complex commercial disputes.

Over the years, Ope has advised a wide range of corporate entities and private clients on investment structuring, mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, real estate and infrastructure transactions, regulatory compliance, and cross-border commercial arrangements. He has led and supported negotiations on several high-stakes transactions, bringing a strategic and solution-driven approach that balances legal risk with commercial realities.

In addition to his transactional practice, Ope has substantial litigation experience and has represented clients before various courts and tribunals across Nigeria, including the Federal High Court, the National Industrial Court, and other Superior Courts of Record. His litigation practice spans commercial disputes, labour and employment matters, contractual claims, and regulatory enforcement actions. He is particularly skilled in deploying litigation as a strategic tool in support of broader commercial outcomes.

As Managing Partner, Ope provides the Firm's strategic leadership, overseeing its corporate, commercial, and dispute-resolution practices. He is deeply involved in client matters and is recognised for his analytical depth, negotiation acumen, and ability to craft tailored legal strategies that protect value, close deals, and resolve disputes efficiently. Under his leadership, Strata Partners continues to position itself as a trusted legal and commercial advisory firm.
