About our Company

Trusted legal expertise for businesses and individuals.

Strata Partners is a full-service commercial law firm headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, providing comprehensive legal services to a diverse clientele spanning the private sector, public institutions, multinational corporations, and financial organizations.

Founded on principles of professional excellence and client-centricity, our firm has established itself as a trusted legal advisor to businesses navigating Nigeria's dynamic commercial and regulatory landscape. We combine deep local market knowledge with international best practices, enabling us to serve clients with operations spanning domestic, regional, and global markets.
